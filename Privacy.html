Privacy Policy Effective Date: [03/06/2026] This Privacy Policy explains how Bullsh!t / Bluff Cards (“the App”) handles information when you use the Discord Activity. Information We Collect When you use the App, we may receive basic Discord account information from Discord, including your Discord user ID, username, and display name. We also process gameplay information needed to run the game, such as lobby membership, selected deck style, game actions, turn state, and card-game snapshots. How We Use Information We use this information to: - identify players in the lobby - assign host and client roles - run multiplayer gameplay - show player names during the game - maintain game state while the Activity is running - troubleshoot bugs or abuse if necessary Data Storage The App is intended to operate as a temporary multiplayer game session. We do not intentionally store long-term player profiles, game history, or analytics at this stage. Game session data may pass through our relay server while the Activity is running. Server logs may temporarily include technical information such as request times, connection status, errors, IP addresses, or Discord user IDs for debugging and security. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only when necessary to: - operate the App through Discord - comply with legal obligations - investigate abuse, security issues, or violations of Discord’s Terms of Service Discord The App runs as a Discord Activity. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord’s own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Data Security We use reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. Children The App is not intended for users who are not allowed to use Discord under Discord’s own age requirements or local law. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above.
